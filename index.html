Why We Exist

The missing link in too many homes is presence — we help mothers fill the gap with purpose.

According to Stats SA, the majority of children in South Africa live in non-standard family structures, raised mostly by their mothers alone. A 2021 Human Sciences Research Council survey found that over 60% of children in SA do not live with their biological fathers — a gap linked to violent crime, low education outcomes and mental illness.

By equipping the mothers raising these boys, we help shape a generation of men who make different, better-informed decisions — for themselves, their families, and their communities.

"Empowering mostly the mothers will result in boys growing to become men who make different and better-informed decisions."

Our Mission

Equip every mother with the tools to raise transformational sons.

To empower mothers with the understanding, capacity, compassion and tools they need to raise boys — in a community and environment that enables them to become transformational leaders.

Our Vision

Boys who grow into resilient, accountable men.

Raising boys to become transformational leaders who are psycho-emotionally healthy and resilient, encouraging authenticity and accountability, contributing positively to a healthier society.
